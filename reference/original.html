A bill book and a cash drawer stop working once the shop gets busy

These are the problems shop owners tell us about most, and what changes with Pentarix POS.

Without POSWith Pentarix POS

Cashiers remember prices and add up bills by hand.

Scan the barcode and the price, promotion and total are filled in.

The drawer never matches at closing and nobody knows why.

Each counter session records the opening float, withdrawals, expenses and the variance.

You find out an item ran out or expired when a customer asks for it.

Batches, expiry dates and low stock show on one screen.

Credit written in a notebook goes missing.

Every customer has a credit balance, payment history and loyalty points.

Refunds on returns are guessed, often at the full price.

Returns are checked against the invoice, refunded at what was paid, and put back into stock.

To see sales across branches you phone each one.

Today's sales, top products and alerts for every branch are on one dashboard.

From the supplier's lorry to head office

See stock arrive, sell across several counters, and every branch stay connected.

  1. Suppliers deliver stockThe lorry brings the order and a GRN adds it to stock.
  2. Stock goes on the shelfStaff fill the shelves, with batch and expiry tracked.
  3. Customers shop, many countersCustomers pick their items and go to any open counter.
  4. Promotions and paymentPromotions apply, customers pay by card, cash or credit, and the receipt prints.
  5. Every branch syncsSales reach head office, and low stock sends a purchase order to the supplier.

Everything inside Pentarix POS

Twelve modules in one system, from the counter to head office.

Checkout

  • Barcode, SKU and name search with a touch-friendly cashier screen
  • Scale barcodes read weight or price from the label
  • Sell by pack or piece with unit conversions and variants
  • Sales history, invoice search and order cancellation
  • Part-paid bills with the balance carried on the order

Payments

  • Split one bill across several payment methods
  • Cash, card, bank transfer and cheque
  • Credit sales to a customer account
  • Pay with a return voucher
  • Payment-method mix reported per branch and counter

Sales returns

  • Return against the original invoice, line by line
  • Refund at the net price paid, after promotions
  • Blocks returning more than was sold
  • Unpaid amounts are reduced first; credit goes back to the account
  • Restock as good or damaged, in the right unit

Promotions

  • Item percentage or fixed discounts
  • Bill percentage or fixed discounts
  • Buy X get Y rules
  • Promotions on specific batches, such as near-expiry stock
  • Promo codes at the counter, with start and end dates

Customers & loyalty

  • Customer accounts with credit balance and limits
  • Record payments against what is owed
  • Loyalty points: set points per rupee, earn and redeem
  • Full balance and loyalty history per customer
  • Customer engagement report per branch

Cash & counters

  • Set up counters per branch
  • Open a session with an opening float and note count
  • Record withdrawals and expenses during the day
  • Close with a denomination count and variance
  • Variance alerts and counter performance reports

Inventory

  • Stock per branch with batches and expiry dates
  • Stock adjustments and stock counts
  • Transfers between branches
  • Full movement history for every item
  • Slow-moving products and inventory health

Purchasing & suppliers

  • Suppliers with their item lists and prices
  • Purchase orders and goods received (GRN)
  • Supplier payments and ledger, with unpaid supplies
  • Purchase returns and supplier refunds
  • Spend, receiving and payment-exposure reports

Product catalogue

  • Items with categories, sub-categories and brands
  • Units, pack sizes and variants
  • Tax rates per item
  • Bulk import of the catalogue
  • Price history and an audit log of every change

Reports & dashboards

  • Branch dashboard: today, this month and trends
  • Top and slow-selling products, top promotions
  • Returns, purchases and supplies analytics
  • Items that need attention and notifications
  • Head-office view comparing all branches

Multi-branch

  • Run several branches from one account
  • Each branch keeps its own stock, counters and settings
  • Branch status and performance side by side
  • Per-branch loyalty and scale-barcode settings

Users & security

  • Roles and permissions, from owner to cashier
  • A separate cashier login for the counter
  • Manager authorisation for sensitive actions
  • Audit log of who changed what, and when
